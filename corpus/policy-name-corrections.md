# Name Corrections

Tickets are not transferable, but we can fix genuine mistakes so the name matches your ID.

## Free corrections

- **Typos of up to 3 characters** in total across first and last name (for example "Jonh" to "John", "Smtih" to "Smith") are corrected free of charge.
- Swapped first and last name.
- Adding or removing a middle name.
- Changing a nickname to the legal name (for example "Bill" to "William").
- **Legal name change** after marriage, divorce or court order, with a copy of the legal document.

## Not allowed

Changing the ticket to a different person is not possible on any fare, even within the same family.

## How to request

Corrections of up to 3 characters can be made in the app under **Manage booking > Passenger details > Correct name** until check-in opens. Larger corrections and legal name changes are done by the contact center after verification. Corrections are not possible once a boarding pass has been issued; contact the airport desk instead.
