# Booking and Payment FAQ

**Q: I didn't get a confirmation email.**
A: Check your spam folder first. Confirmations are sent within 15 minutes of payment. If you still have nothing, your booking reference is shown on the final payment screen and in **Trips** if you were signed in. You can resend the confirmation from **Manage booking > Resend confirmation**.

**Q: Was I charged twice?**
A: When a payment fails and you retry, your bank may show a temporary authorization for the failed attempt. It disappears within 3 to 5 business days. Only one charge is completed per booking. If two completed charges appear after 5 business days, contact us before contacting your bank.

**Q: Can I book for someone else?**
A: Yes. Enter the traveller's name exactly as on their ID. You become the purchaser and can manage the booking.

**Q: Do children pay full fare?**
A: Children aged 2 and over pay the adult fare. See the Travelling with Kids FAQ for infants.

**Q: Can I hold a fare without paying?**
A: No, SkyDesk does not offer fare holds.

**Q: Why did the price change while I was booking?**
A: Fares are dynamic and seats at a given price can sell out. The price is guaranteed once you reach the payment step, for 20 minutes.

**Q: Can I book a one-way ticket?**
A: Yes, all SkyDesk fares are priced per direction, so one-way tickets cost half of a comparable return.
