# Travel Documents and ID Requirements

You are responsible for having the right travel documents. SkyDesk staff check documents at check-in and at the gate, and we must deny boarding to customers without valid documents. Fares are not refunded in that case.

## Domestic flights (within the United States)

Adults aged 18 and over need a REAL ID-compliant driver's licence or state ID, a passport, or another TSA-accepted photo ID. Children under 18 travelling with an adult do not need ID on domestic flights.

## International flights

- A passport valid for at least **6 months beyond your return date** is required for all SkyDesk international destinations, even where the destination country itself accepts less.
- You may need a visa or electronic travel authorization. Check the destination's government website before booking; SkyDesk cannot issue visas or advise on eligibility.
- Online check-in for international flights requires you to enter passport details in the app. Documents are checked again at the airport.

## Name must match

The name on the booking must match the ID you travel with. Middle names may be omitted. Small spelling mistakes can be fixed; see the Name Corrections policy.

## Minors travelling with one parent

For international travel, SkyDesk recommends a consent letter signed by the non-travelling parent. Some destinations require it.
