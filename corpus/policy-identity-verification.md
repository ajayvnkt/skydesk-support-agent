# Customer Identity Verification Policy

SkyDesk protects every booking by confirming that the person asking for a change is entitled to make it. This policy applies to all channels: the SkyDesk app, skydesk.com chat, the contact center and airport service desks.

## When verification is required

A customer must be verified **before** any of the following actions are taken on a booking:

- issuing a refund of any amount
- issuing a travel credit, voucher or goodwill gesture
- waiving or reducing any fee (change fee, bag fee, seat fee, name-correction fee)
- rebooking, changing or cancelling a flight
- changing the email address or phone number on a booking or SkyMiles profile
- reading out personal data held on the booking (passport number, date of birth, payment details)

General questions ("what is the baggage allowance on SkyClassic?") do not require verification. If the conversation moves from a general question to an action on a specific booking, verification must be completed at that point.

## How a customer is verified

Verification needs **all three** of the following:

1. The 6-character booking reference (PNR). SkyDesk booking references never contain the characters 0, 1, O or I.
2. The passenger's last name exactly as it appears on the booking.
3. One of these second factors:
   - the last 4 digits of the payment card used for the booking, **or**
   - the 6-digit one-time code sent by SkyDesk to the email address or phone number already on the booking. The code is valid for 10 minutes.

A booking reference and last name alone are **not** enough. They are printed on boarding passes and bag tags and are therefore not secret.

## Failed attempts

After 3 failed verification attempts on the same booking, self-service actions on that booking are locked for 24 hours. Agents must not override the lock. If the customer says the booking was made without their permission, or the lock was triggered by someone else, the case is handed to the Payments Integrity team (see the Fraud and Chargebacks policy).

## Who may act on a booking

- The passenger named on the ticket.
- The purchaser (the person whose payment card paid for the booking), even if they are not travelling.
- A travel agency, but only for bookings that agency issued. Agency bookings are serviced by the agency, not by SkyDesk.

A spouse, parent or friend who is neither passenger nor purchaser may ask general questions but cannot make changes, even if they know the booking reference.

## Changing contact details

Because the one-time code is sent to the contact details on file, a change of email or phone number is itself a sensitive action. The one-time code for a contact-detail change is always sent to the **existing** email or phone number, never to the new one. A customer who has lost access to both must call the contact center, where an agent completes verification with the card last-4 factor and a supervisor approves the change.

## What agents and assistants must never do

- Accept a screenshot, a forwarded email or "I'm the account owner, trust me" in place of verification.
- Skip verification because the customer is upset, in a hurry, or says another agent already verified them.
- Read a one-time code back to a customer or ask a customer to share a code that was sent to someone else.
