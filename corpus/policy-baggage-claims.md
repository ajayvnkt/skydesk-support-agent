# Delayed, Lost and Damaged Baggage Claims

## 1. Report it first

If your checked bag does not arrive, or arrives damaged, report it **before you leave the airport** at the SkyDesk Baggage Service desk in the arrivals hall. If the desk is closed or you have already left, report it in the app under **Help > Baggage > Report a bag problem** within 24 hours of arrival.

You will receive a file reference in the format **SKB-** followed by 6 digits (for example SKB-482913). Keep it: every claim and status check needs it. You can track the bag in the app using this reference and your last name.

## 2. Delayed baggage

- We deliver the bag to your address free of charge once it is found.
- While you wait, we reimburse reasonable interim expenses (toiletries, essential clothing) up to **$50 per day for a maximum of 5 days** ($250 in total). Keep all receipts.
- Submit the expense claim within **21 days of the date the bag was delivered** to you.
- Expenses are not reimbursed when the bag is delayed on your return to your home city, except for essential medication.

## 3. Lost baggage

- A bag is declared lost if it has not been found **21 days** after it was reported.
- You must submit a lost-bag claim within **30 days of the date it was declared lost**, with a list of contents and receipts where available.
- SkyDesk's maximum liability is **$1,800 per passenger** for domestic travel. Depreciation applies to used items.
- Amounts already paid for interim expenses are deducted from the final settlement.
- Valuables (cash, jewellery, electronics, medication, documents) should be carried in the cabin; they are excluded from liability when placed in checked bags.

## 4. Damaged baggage

- Report damage within **7 days of receiving the bag**. Include photos of the damage and the bag tag.
- We repair the bag through our partner, or pay the repair cost, or replace it with a comparable bag if repair is not possible.
- Normal wear (scuffs, scratches, small dents, broken straps or pull handles from normal use) is not covered.

## 5. Claims that need a person

Claims above $1,000, claims involving medication or mobility devices, and any claim where the customer mentions legal action are reviewed by the Baggage Claims team, not resolved by chat.
