# Refunds and Credits FAQ

**Q: How do I check the status of my refund?**
A: In the app go to **Trips > Past and cancelled > your trip > Refund status**. The status shows Requested, Approved or Paid. Once it shows Paid, the money is with your bank; card refunds can take a further few days to appear on a statement.

**Q: I got a travel credit but wanted cash. Can it be converted?**
A: No, travel credits cannot be converted to cash. The fare rules shown at booking decide whether a cancellation gives a refund or a credit.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I'm sick and can't fly?**
A: The normal rules for your fare class apply. SkyFlex and SkyBusiness are refundable. For other fares, a change-fee waiver for a documented medical emergency may be possible with supervisor approval; contact an agent.

**Q: The flight price dropped after I booked. Can I get the difference?**
A: Not as cash. If you are within the 24-hour risk-free window you can cancel and rebook at the lower price. SkyFlex and SkyBusiness customers can change to the same flight at the lower fare and receive the difference as travel credit.

**Q: I paid for a seat but was moved. Do I get my seat fee back?**
A: Yes, if you were moved to a lower seat type. The refund is processed automatically within 7 business days.

**Q: Why was my refund less than I expected?**
A: Common reasons: part of the booking was paid with a travel credit (that part comes back as credit), a SkyClassic cancellation fee was deducted from the credit, or only taxes were refundable on a SkyLite ticket.

**Q: Can my refund go to a different card?**
A: No, refunds go to the original form of payment only.
