# Changing or Cancelling a Booking Online

## Find your booking

Open **Trips** in the app or **Manage booking** on skydesk.com and enter your booking reference and last name. If you see error PNR-404, see the App and Website Error Codes page.

## Change a flight

1. Tap **Change flight**.
2. Choose the flight(s) to change and search for new dates or times.
3. The price screen shows the change fee for your fare class and any fare difference. The total is shown before you pay.
4. Confirm and pay. A new confirmation email and new boarding passes (if checked in) are issued.

For SkyLite bookings the **Change flight** button is greyed out with the message "Changes are not available for this fare".

## Cancel a booking

1. Tap **Cancel booking**.
2. A preview screen shows exactly what you will get back: a refund to your card, a travel credit, or taxes only, depending on your fare class and whether you are inside the 24-hour risk-free window.
3. Tick the box to confirm and tap **Cancel booking**.

You receive a cancellation email with the refund amount or the travel credit code.

## Things you can't do online

- Bookings with an unaccompanied minor or a pet.
- Group bookings (10 or more passengers).
- Bookings made through a travel agent.
- Name corrections of more than 3 characters.

Contact us for these.
