# Baggage FAQ

**Q: Can I take liquids in my cabin bag?**
A: Yes, in containers of up to 100 ml each, all fitting in one clear resealable bag of about 1 litre. Baby food, breast milk and prescribed medicines are allowed in larger amounts. Liquids bought in the airport after security are allowed.

**Q: Can I put power banks or spare lithium batteries in my checked bag?**
A: No. Power banks, e-cigarettes and spare lithium batteries must be carried in the cabin only. Power banks up to 100 Wh are allowed without approval; 100 to 160 Wh need approval from the airport desk; over 160 Wh are not allowed. Batteries for mobility aids follow the Special Assistance rules.

**Q: My cabin bag is a little bigger than allowed. Will it be accepted?**
A: Cabin bags are measured at the gate. If it does not fit the sizer it will be checked in at the gate and the airport checked-bag price is charged.

**Q: Can two of us share one checked bag allowance?**
A: No. Allowances are per passenger and weight cannot be pooled.

**Q: Can I pay for an extra bag with miles?**
A: Yes, bags can be paid with SkyMiles in the app.

**Q: How do I check in a guitar?**
A: Small instruments can travel as your cabin bag if they fit the overhead bin. Larger instruments can be checked as a regular bag (oversize fees may apply), or you can buy an extra seat for the instrument by calling the contact center.

**Q: Where do I collect my bags on a connecting flight?**
A: On domestic connections bags are checked through to your final destination. On international arrivals into the US you collect your bags at the first US airport, clear customs, and re-drop them at the connection bag drop.

**Q: My bag didn't arrive, what do I do?**
A: Go to the Baggage Service desk in the arrivals hall before leaving the airport. See Delayed, Lost and Damaged Baggage Claims for the full process and deadlines.
