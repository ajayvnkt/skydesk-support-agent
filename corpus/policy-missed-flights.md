# Missed Flights and No-Show

## If you are running late

Check-in and bag drop deadlines are strict. If you think you will miss check-in, contact us **before** the departure time. A flight cancelled before departure is treated under your fare's normal change and refund rules; after departure it becomes a no-show.

## The 2-hour late arrival rule

If you arrive at the airport after your flight has left but within **2 hours** of its scheduled departure, you can be placed on standby for the next SkyDesk flight to the same destination on the same day, free of charge. This applies to SkyClassic, SkyFlex and SkyBusiness. SkyLite customers are not eligible and must buy a new ticket.

## What happens to the rest of your trip

If you do not take a flight and did not cancel it, **all remaining flights on the booking are cancelled automatically**, including your return. If you still want to use the return flight, tell us before the missed flight departs.

## What you get back

See "No-shows" in the Refunds and Cancellations Policy for what each fare class receives.

## Missed connections

If you miss a connection because your earlier SkyDesk flight was late, this is not a no-show: we rebook you automatically on the next available flight, and the Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy applies.
