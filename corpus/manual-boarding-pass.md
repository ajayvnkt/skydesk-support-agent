# Boarding Passes and Boarding Groups

## Getting your boarding pass

After check-in your boarding pass is available:

- in the app under **Trips > Boarding pass** (works offline once downloaded),
- in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet,
- as a PDF by email to print at home,
- printed at an airport kiosk or desk.

Screenshots of the boarding pass are accepted at security and at the gate if the QR code is clearly visible.

## Boarding groups

| Group | Who |
|---|---|
| Pre-boarding | Customers needing assistance, families with children under 2 |
| Group 1 | SkyBusiness, SkyMiles Platinum |
| Group 2 | SkyFlex, SkyMiles Gold |
| Group 3 | SkyClassic rows at the back of the aircraft |
| Group 4 | SkyClassic remaining rows |
| Group 5 | SkyLite |

Your group is printed on the boarding pass. Boarding usually starts 40 minutes before departure.

## Lost phone or pass

Go to any kiosk with your passport or booking reference and reprint it free of charge.
