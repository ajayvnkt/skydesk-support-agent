# Delays and Disruptions FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my flight is delayed?**
A: We send push notifications, SMS or email (whichever you chose in Profile > Notifications). You can also check **Flight status** in the app.

**Q: My flight is delayed because of weather. Do I get compensation?**
A: Weather is outside SkyDesk's control, so no compensation is paid, but you can be rebooked free of charge. See the Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy for what counts as controllable.

**Q: My flight was cancelled. Will you rebook me automatically?**
A: Yes, we rebook you on the next available flight and send the new itinerary. If it doesn't suit you, choose another option in **Trips > your trip > Rebooking options** without charge.

**Q: I booked a hotel at my destination and missed the first night. Will SkyDesk pay?**
A: No, prepaid arrangements at the destination are not covered. Travel insurance may cover this.

**Q: Where can I see the old compensation amounts I was told about?**
A: Compensation amounts changed on 1 July 2026. Only the current policy applies to flights from that date.

**Q: My delay compensation credit hasn't arrived.**
A: Credits are sent automatically. If nothing has arrived within the time stated in the policy, claim it in the app under Help > Delay compensation.
