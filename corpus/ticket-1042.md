# Ticket #1042: bag not arrived (Hinglish)

**Channel:** in-app chat | **Fare:** SkyFlex | **Route:** EWR to DEL (partner connection)

**Customer message:**
> bhai mera bag nahi aaya Delhi mein, flight SD 812 se aaya tha. airport se nikal gaya hu ab, ghar pe hu. kya karu?? mere sare kapde usme the, kal meeting hai

**Agent resolution:**
Replied in English as the channel supports. The customer had already left the airport, so the report was made in the app under Help > Baggage > Report a bag problem, which is allowed within 24 hours of arrival. Report created with file reference SKB-570231. Explained that while the bag is delayed, essential purchases (toiletries, clothing for the meeting) are reimbursed up to $50 per day for up to 5 days with receipts, and that the bag will be delivered to the home address free of charge once found. Bag was located in Newark and delivered two days later.

**Resolution code:** RES-PIR
