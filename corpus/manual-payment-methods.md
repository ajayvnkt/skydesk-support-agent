# Accepted Payment Methods

## Online (app and skydesk.com)

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit and debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- SkyDesk travel credits (codes starting with TC-)
- SkyMiles

Prices are charged in US dollars. Your bank may add a foreign transaction fee if your card is in another currency.

## Splitting a payment

You can combine up to 3 travel credits and one other payment method in a single booking. Two different cards cannot be combined.

## Airport

Card and mobile wallets only at airport desks and gates. Cash is not accepted.

## Holds

SkyDesk does not offer fare holds. Once you reach the payment step, your fare and seats are held for 20 minutes.

## Receipts and invoices

Your receipt is the confirmation email. A tax invoice with company details can be downloaded from **Manage booking > Receipts** for up to 12 months after travel.

## Payment problems

See SD-ERR-112 on the App and Website Error Codes page.
