# Fraud, Unauthorized Bookings and Chargebacks

These cases are always handled by the **Payments Integrity team**. Chat assistants and frontline agents do not resolve them.

## Escalate immediately when the customer mentions

- a **chargeback** or a dispute they have opened (or plan to open) with their bank or card issuer,
- a booking or charge they say they did not make or authorize,
- a stolen card, or someone else using their card,
- someone else getting into their SkyMiles account (account takeover), missing miles they did not redeem, or a changed email they did not request,
- being contacted by someone pretending to be SkyDesk and asking for codes or card details.

## What to do while handing over

- Do **not** issue any refund, credit or voucher on the booking. If a chargeback is open and SkyDesk also refunds, the customer is refunded twice and the second refund has to be clawed back.
- Do **not** cancel or change the booking unless the Payments Integrity team instructs it.
- Do **not** ask the customer to read out full card numbers or one-time codes in chat.
- Tell the customer the case has been passed to a specialist team, which responds within **2 business days** by email.

## For customers

If you see a SkyDesk charge you do not recognise, contact us before contacting your bank: many unrecognised charges are seat or bag purchases made by a travel companion on the same booking. If you have already opened a dispute with your bank, let us know the dispute reference so that we can work with your bank directly. Opening a chargeback freezes all changes and refunds on the booking until the dispute is closed.

SkyDesk will never ask you for your password, full card number or a one-time code by phone, email or social media.
