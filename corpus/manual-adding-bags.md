# Adding Bags in the App

Buying bags in advance is cheaper than paying at the airport. Online prices are available until 4 hours before departure; the prices themselves are listed on the Baggage Fees page.

## Steps

1. Go to **Trips > your trip > Manage booking > Bags**.
2. Each passenger's included allowance is shown first (for example "1 x 23 kg included").
3. Tap **+** next to Checked bag or Cabin bag for the passenger. The price for that extra bag appears.
4. If you are travelling on a return booking, choose whether to add the bag on the outbound, return or both.
5. Tap **Continue to payment**.

You will receive an updated confirmation email showing the new allowance. The bag is also shown on your boarding pass.

## Good to know

- You can add bags after check-in until 4 hours before departure. After that, bags are bought at the airport.
- Bags cannot be removed after purchase, and bag fees are not refunded if you do not use them.
- Weight is not pooled between passengers: two passengers with one 23 kg bag each cannot check one 46 kg bag.
- If you see error BAG-301, see the App and Website Error Codes page.
