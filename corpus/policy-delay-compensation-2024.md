# Delay Compensation Policy (archived)

> **SUPERSEDED.** This page is kept for reference only. It was replaced on 1 July 2026 by the current Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy (policy-delay-compensation). Do not quote these amounts to customers.

## Compensation for controllable delays (2024 rules)

Delay length was measured from the scheduled **departure** time.

| Departure delay | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 3 hours or more | $50 travel credit |
| 4 hours or more | Meal voucher of $10 |
| 5 hours or more | $150 travel credit |

Credits under this version were valid for 6 months.

## Claims

Customers had to email delays@skydesk.example within 14 days of the flight to claim. This mailbox is no longer monitored.

## Why it changed

The 2024 rules measured delay from departure, which meant customers whose flights made up time in the air could receive compensation while arriving almost on time, and customers delayed after pushback received nothing. The 2026 policy measures delay at arrival instead.
