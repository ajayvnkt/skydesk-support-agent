# Baggage Allowance by Fare Class

Your included baggage depends on the fare class shown on your booking confirmation. Allowances are per passenger, per direction.

## Personal item (all fares)

Every passenger, on every fare, may bring one personal item that fits under the seat in front: maximum **40 x 30 x 20 cm** (for example a handbag, small backpack or laptop bag).

## Included allowance

| Fare class | Cabin bag (overhead bin) | Checked bags |
|---|---|---|
| SkyLite | Not included (personal item only) | None included |
| SkyClassic | 1 cabin bag, max 7 kg | 1 checked bag, max 23 kg |
| SkyFlex | 1 cabin bag, max 10 kg | 2 checked bags, max 23 kg each |
| SkyBusiness | 2 cabin bags, max 10 kg each | 2 checked bags, max 32 kg each |

## Size limits

- **Cabin bag:** maximum **55 x 40 x 23 cm** including wheels and handles.
- **Checked bag:** maximum **158 cm** in total linear dimensions (length + width + height).
- No single checked bag may weigh more than 32 kg on any fare.

Bags above the included weight or size, and extra bags, are charged as described in the Baggage Fees policy. SkyMiles members with elite status may have an additional allowance; see the SkyMiles programme page.

## Infants

A lap infant (under 2) has no bag allowance of their own, but one stroller and one car seat can be checked free of charge for each infant.

## Items that do not count against the allowance

Mobility aids and medical devices do not count against the allowance (see the Special Assistance page). A pet carrier travelling in the cabin replaces the cabin bag for that passenger (see the Pets page).

## Where the allowance is checked

Cabin bags are measured and weighed at the boarding gate. A SkyLite customer who brings a cabin bag to the gate without having bought one is charged the gate price.
