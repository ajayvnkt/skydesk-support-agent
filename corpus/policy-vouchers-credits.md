# Vouchers and Travel Credits

SkyDesk issues two kinds of value to customers: **travel credits** (used to buy new flights) and **meal vouchers** (used at the airport). This page explains how both work.

## Travel credits

Travel credits come from SkyClassic cancellations, no-show conversions on SkyFlex and SkyBusiness, delay compensation, goodwill gestures and denied-boarding volunteers.

- **Validity:** a travel credit is valid for **12 months from the date it was issued**. The new booking must be made before the credit expires; the travel itself can take place after the expiry date.
- **Who can use it:** the credit is in the name of the passenger it was issued to. It can pay for flights for that passenger or for other passengers on the same new booking, as long as the credit holder is also travelling on it.
- **No cash:** travel credits cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred to another person, or sold.
- **Combining:** up to **3 travel credits** can be combined on a single booking, together with one card payment for any balance.
- **What they pay for:** fares, taxes, seats and bags. They cannot pay for SkyMiles purchases or third-party insurance.
- **Leftover value:** if the new booking costs less than the credit, the remaining value stays as a credit with the **original** expiry date.
- **Finding a credit:** credits are sent by email with a code starting with **TC-**, and appear in the app under **Wallet**.

### Expired credits

A credit that has expired can be extended **once**, by **3 months**, if the customer asks within **30 days after the expiry date**. Extensions are processed by a contact center agent after verification; the chat assistant cannot extend credits. Requests made more than 30 days after expiry are declined.

## Meal vouchers

- Meal vouchers are valid **only on the day they are issued**, at airport food outlets that display the SkyDesk partner sign.
- They cannot be used on board, are not refundable, and no change is given for unused value.
- A meal voucher is per passenger and cannot be pooled across passengers.

## Lost or unreceived credits

If a customer cannot find a credit, the agent can resend it to the email on the booking after verification. A credit is never resent to a different email address than the one on file.
