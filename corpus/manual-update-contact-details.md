# Updating Your Contact Details

Keeping your email and phone number up to date is how we reach you about delays, gate changes and schedule changes.

## On your SkyMiles profile

1. Tap **Profile > Personal details > Contact**.
2. Tap **Edit** next to the email address or phone number.
3. Enter the new details and tap **Send code**.
4. A 6-digit one-time code is sent to your **current** email or phone (not the new one) to confirm it is really you. Enter it within 10 minutes.
5. The change is confirmed by a message to both the old and the new address.

## On a single booking

If you booked without a SkyMiles account, or want a different contact for one trip (for example a family member travelling), go to **Manage booking > Contact info**. The same one-time code process applies, sent to the contact currently on that booking.

## I no longer have access to my old email or phone

You cannot change it in the app. Contact us by phone; an agent will verify you using your booking reference, last name and the last 4 digits of the card used to pay, and a supervisor approves the change. This protects your account from takeover.

## Notifications

Under **Profile > Notifications** you can choose push notifications, SMS or email for flight updates. Turning off all three means you might miss a schedule change.
