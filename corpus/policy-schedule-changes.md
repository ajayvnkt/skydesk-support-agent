# Schedule Changes Policy

A schedule change is a change to a flight time, flight number or routing that SkyDesk makes in advance, usually because of timetable planning. It is different from a same-day delay, which is covered by the Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy.

## How you are told

We email and send an app notification to the contact details on the booking. Please keep these up to date in Manage booking.

## Your options

| Size of the change | Your options |
|---|---|
| Less than 90 minutes | The new time is confirmed automatically. Normal fare rules apply if you want to change or cancel. |
| 90 minutes or more (earlier or later), a new connection, or a change of airport | Accept the new time, **or** move free of charge to another SkyDesk flight departing within 3 days before or after the original date on the same route, **or** cancel for a full refund to the original form of payment. |

These options apply to **every fare class, including SkyLite**, and no change fee or fare difference is charged when you move to another flight under this policy.

## Time limits

- You must choose an option before the original departure time.
- If you do not respond, the new schedule is treated as accepted once you check in.

## Seats and extras

Paid seats are reassigned to a comparable seat. If no comparable seat exists on the new flight, the seat fee is refunded. Bags, pets and other extras move with you to the new flight.

## Connections

If a schedule change makes a connection shorter than the minimum connection time (45 minutes domestic, 75 minutes international), SkyDesk will rebook the connection automatically and notify you.
