# Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy

Effective 1 July 2026. This version replaces the March 2024 policy, which must no longer be quoted.

This policy covers what SkyDesk provides when a flight is delayed or cancelled on the day of travel or shortly before. For planned timetable changes announced in advance, see the Schedule Changes policy.

## Controllable vs uncontrollable disruptions

Compensation is owed only when the cause was within SkyDesk's control.

- **Controllable:** aircraft maintenance, crew scheduling, late inbound aircraft caused by an earlier controllable event, IT outages, catering or cleaning delays, baggage loading problems.
- **Uncontrollable:** weather, air traffic control restrictions, airport closures, security threats, medical emergencies on board, strikes by third parties (airport or air-traffic staff).

For uncontrollable disruptions SkyDesk rebooks customers free of charge on the next available SkyDesk flight, but no compensation, meal vouchers or hotel are provided.

## Compensation tiers for controllable delays

Delay length is measured at the **arrival** gate at your final destination, compared with the scheduled arrival time on your ticket.

| Arrival delay | What each passenger receives |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 hours | Nothing |
| 2 hours to under 3 hours | $15 meal voucher |
| 3 hours to under 5 hours | $100 travel credit, or 7,500 SkyMiles if the customer prefers |
| 5 hours or more | $250 travel credit, or the option to not travel and receive a full refund of the unused portion |

The tiers are not cumulative: a 6-hour delay gets the 5-hour tier, not the 3-hour tier plus the 5-hour tier. A customer who takes the full refund at 5 hours or more does not also receive the $250 travel credit.

## Overnight delays

If a controllable delay or cancellation means the customer cannot continue until the next day, SkyDesk provides a hotel room and ground transport to and from the hotel, in addition to the tier above. Customers who arrange their own hotel can claim up to $180 per room per night with receipts.

## Controllable cancellations

If SkyDesk cancels a flight for a controllable reason within 14 days of departure, the customer may choose either:

- free rebooking on the next available SkyDesk flight **plus** a $250 travel credit, or
- a full refund of the unused portion of the ticket to the original form of payment.

This applies to every fare class, including SkyLite.

## How compensation is issued

- Meal vouchers are issued at the airport (digital voucher in the app or printed at the gate).
- Travel credits and SkyMiles are issued automatically within 72 hours of arrival to the email on the booking.
- If nothing has arrived after 72 hours, the customer can claim through **Help > Delay compensation** in the app within 30 days of the flight. Claims after 30 days are not accepted.
- Compensation credits follow the Vouchers and Travel Credits policy, including expiry.
- Issuing or adjusting compensation for a specific booking requires customer verification.

## What is not covered

Missed connections with other airlines on separate tickets, prepaid hotels or events at the destination, and lost earnings are not covered by this policy.
