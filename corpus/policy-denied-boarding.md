# Overbooking and Denied Boarding

Occasionally more customers hold confirmed seats than there are seats on the aircraft.

## Volunteers first

Before anyone is denied boarding, gate staff ask for volunteers. Volunteers receive a travel credit (the starting offer is **$300**, and it may increase), a confirmed seat on a later flight and, if needed, a meal voucher and hotel.

## Involuntary denied boarding (domestic flights)

If not enough people volunteer and you are denied boarding against your will, you are entitled to compensation paid by check or to the original form of payment, unless you accept a travel credit instead:

| Arrival delay at destination compared with original flight | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1 hour | No compensation |
| 1 to 2 hours | 200% of the one-way fare, maximum $775 |
| More than 2 hours | 400% of the one-way fare, maximum $1,550 |

## Who is not entitled

Customers who did not check in on time, did not meet document requirements, were downgraded rather than removed, or were moved for safety or aircraft-weight reasons on aircraft with 30 or fewer seats.

## Order of selection

Customers who have checked in last are selected first, except customers with disabilities, unaccompanied minors and families travelling together.
