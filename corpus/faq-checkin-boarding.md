# Check-in and Airport FAQ

**Q: How early should I arrive at the airport?**
A: We recommend 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights. The firm deadlines are on the Check-in & Boarding Times page.

**Q: Do I need to print my boarding pass?**
A: No, the mobile boarding pass in the app or your wallet is enough.

**Q: Can I check in for my return flight at the same time?**
A: Only if the return departs within 24 hours. Otherwise check in again when check-in opens.

**Q: Is there a fee to check in at the airport desk?**
A: No, airport check-in is free on every fare.

**Q: I have TSA PreCheck. How do I add my Known Traveler Number?**
A: In **Manage booking > Passenger details > Secure flight info**. Add it at least 24 hours before departure so it prints on your boarding pass.

**Q: My boarding pass says "SSSS". What does it mean?**
A: You have been selected for additional security screening by the TSA. SkyDesk cannot remove this; allow extra time at security.

**Q: I'm in a wheelchair. Do I need to arrive earlier?**
A: Please arrive at least 2 hours before departure and make sure assistance was requested in advance (see Special Assistance).
