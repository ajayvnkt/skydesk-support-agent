# Ticket #1048: BAG-301 when adding bag

**Channel:** in-app chat | **Fare:** SkyClassic

**Customer message:**
> app gives BAG-301 allowance exceeded when i put 35kg for my suitcase. i can pay extra no problem just let me

**Agent resolution:**
Explained that no checked bag over 32 kg is accepted on any fare, which is why the app returns BAG-301 for a 35 kg weight; paying extra is not possible. Suggested splitting the contents into two bags. The SkyClassic fare includes one 23 kg bag; the customer bought a 1st extra checked bag online for $40. Both bags were kept under 23 kg, so no overweight fee applied.

**Resolution code:** RES-INFO
