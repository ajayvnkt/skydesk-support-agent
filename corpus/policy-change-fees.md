# Flight Change Fees by Fare Class

A voluntary change means the customer chooses to move to a different date, time or route. For changes caused by SkyDesk, see the Schedule Changes policy and the Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy, where changes are free.

## Change fees

| Fare class | Change fee | Fare difference |
|---|---|---|
| SkyLite | Changes not permitted | n/a |
| SkyClassic | $75 per passenger, per direction | Customer pays any increase; a lower new fare is not refunded |
| SkyFlex | $0 | Customer pays any increase; a lower new fare is returned as travel credit |
| SkyBusiness | $0 | Customer pays any increase; a lower new fare is returned as travel credit |

- SkyLite tickets cannot be changed. The only options are cancelling within the 24-hour risk-free window and booking again, or keeping the original flight.
- Changes must be made at least **2 hours before the scheduled departure**. After that, only same-day changes (below) are possible.
- A change of passenger (giving the ticket to someone else) is never permitted on any fare. Spelling fixes are handled under the Name Corrections policy.
- Changing the route is allowed on SkyClassic, SkyFlex and SkyBusiness, priced as a new fare plus the change fee for SkyClassic.

## Same-day changes

From 24 hours before departure you can move to an earlier or later flight on the **same day and same route**:

| Fare class | Same-day change |
|---|---|
| SkyLite | Not available |
| SkyClassic | $50, no fare difference |
| SkyFlex | Free |
| SkyBusiness | Free |

Same-day changes are subject to seat availability and must be made before the original flight's check-in closes.

## Fee waivers

Agents may waive a change fee only in the situations listed in the agent conduct guidelines (for example a documented medical emergency approved by a supervisor). A waiver always requires customer verification first.
