# Ticket #1047: "I disputed it with my bank, now refund me"

**Channel:** email

**Customer message:**
> I filed a chargeback with Chase last week bc you charged me $89 for bags I never added. Just refund it now and I'll cancel the dispute. Also someone changed my email on my SkyMiles?? I didn't do that.

**Agent resolution:**
Did not issue a refund because a chargeback was open (a refund would pay the customer twice). The message also reported an email change the customer did not request, a possible account takeover. Escalated to Payments Integrity with a neutral summary, and told the customer the specialist team would reply by email within 2 business days. Advised the customer never to share one-time codes.

**Resolution code:** ESC-PAY
