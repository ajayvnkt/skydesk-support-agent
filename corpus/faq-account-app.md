# Account and App FAQ

**Q: I forgot my password.**
A: Tap **Sign in > Forgot password**. A reset link is sent to your profile email and expires after 1 hour.

**Q: The app keeps logging me out.**
A: Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity (error ACC-401). Turn on **Stay signed in** on a personal device.

**Q: Which phones does the app support?**
A: iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, under **Profile > Privacy > Delete account**. Your miles are lost when the account is deleted, and the deletion cannot be undone after 14 days.

**Q: I'm not receiving one-time codes.**
A: Check that the phone or email on your profile is correct and look in spam. If you requested too many codes, wait 15 minutes (error OTP-429). If your contact details are out of date, see Updating Your Contact Details.

**Q: Can I use the app in Spanish?**
A: Yes, the app follows your phone's language for English, Spanish and French.
