# SkyMiles FAQ

**Q: I forgot to add my SkyMiles number. Can I still get the miles?**
A: Yes, claim missing miles in the app under **SkyMiles > Missing miles** with your booking reference. See the SkyMiles programme page for the time limit.

**Q: Can I transfer miles to my partner?**
A: Yes, up to 50,000 miles per calendar year can be transferred to another SkyMiles member for a fee of 1 cent per mile.

**Q: Can I merge two accounts?**
A: No. If you have accidentally opened two accounts, contact us and we will move the miles into one and close the other.

**Q: Do I earn miles on award tickets?**
A: No, flights paid fully with miles do not earn miles.

**Q: Does my status carry over to next year?**
A: Status earned in one calendar year is valid until 31 January of the year after next. For example, Gold earned in 2026 lasts until 31 January 2028.

**Q: Can I buy miles?**
A: Yes, in blocks of 1,000 miles for $35 per block, up to 30,000 miles per year.
