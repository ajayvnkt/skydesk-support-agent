# Ticket #1043: cancel SkyLite booked last night

**Channel:** web chat | **Fare:** SkyLite

**Customer message:**
> I booked the wrong month by mistake last night (Oct instead of Nov!!). It's SkyLite which says non refundable. am I just stuck?

**Agent resolution:**
Checked the booking timestamp: booked 14 hours earlier, and the flight was 6 weeks away, so both conditions for the 24-hour risk-free cancellation were met. After verification, cancelled the booking for a full refund to the original card including the seat bought in the same transaction. The customer then made a new booking for November themselves. Explained the refund takes up to 7 business days to reach the card.

**Resolution code:** RES-REFUND
