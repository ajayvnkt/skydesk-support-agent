# 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation

Plans change. Every SkyDesk ticket comes with a 24-hour risk-free window.

## The rule

You can cancel any booking within 24 hours of making it and receive a full refund to your original form of payment, whatever the fare class, including SkyLite, as long as **both** of these are true:

1. You cancel within 24 hours of the time the booking was made (the timestamp on your confirmation email).
2. The booking was made at least 7 days before the first flight's departure.

No cancellation fee applies and the refund includes seats and bags bought in the same transaction.

## When the window does not apply

- Bookings made less than 7 days before departure.
- Bookings made through a travel agency or online travel site. Contact the agency instead.
- Group bookings of 10 or more passengers, which follow their group contract.
- Changes. The 24-hour window covers cancellation for a refund only. To travel on a different date you can cancel within the window and make a new booking at the current price.

Once the 24 hours have passed, the normal Refunds and Cancellations Policy for your fare class applies.

## How to cancel

In the app or on skydesk.com go to **Manage booking > Cancel booking**. The cancellation screen shows "Risk-free cancellation: full refund" when you qualify. Refunds are then processed in the normal processing times.
