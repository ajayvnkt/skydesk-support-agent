# SkyDesk Fare Classes Explained

SkyDesk sells four fare classes. Your fare class is printed on your booking confirmation and shown in the app under **Manage booking > Ticket details**. It decides your baggage, change and refund rules.

## SkyLite

Our lowest price for travellers with just a small bag. A personal item only, no changes after the 24-hour window, non-refundable. Seat assigned at check-in. Boards last.

## SkyClassic

Our most popular fare. Includes a cabin bag and one checked bag, standard seat selection, and changes for a fee. Cancellation gives a travel credit rather than a refund.

## SkyFlex

For people whose plans may change. Free changes, fully refundable to the original payment method, two checked bags and a larger cabin bag allowance, standard and preferred seats included, and free same-day changes.

## SkyBusiness

Our premium cabin with wider seats, priority check-in and security, lounge access where available, two cabin bags and two heavy checked bags, free changes and full refunds. Boards first.

## Upgrading your fare

You can upgrade from SkyLite or SkyClassic to SkyFlex before check-in by paying the current fare difference in **Manage booking > Upgrade fare**. A fare cannot be downgraded.
