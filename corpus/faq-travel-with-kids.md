# Travelling with Kids FAQ

**Q: Does my baby need a ticket?**
A: Infants under 2 can travel on an adult's lap. On domestic flights a lap infant is free. On international flights a lap infant pays 10% of the adult fare plus taxes. Each adult can hold one lap infant.

**Q: Can my baby have their own seat?**
A: Yes, if you buy a child ticket. An FAA-approved car seat can then be used on board.

**Q: How old must a baby be to fly?**
A: At least 7 days old. Babies under 14 days need a doctor's fit-to-fly letter.

**Q: My child turns 2 during the trip.**
A: They can travel as a lap infant on the outbound but need their own seat (child ticket) for any flight on or after their 2nd birthday.

**Q: Can my 10-year-old fly alone?**
A: Yes, using our Unaccompanied Minor service, which is mandatory at that age. See the Children Travelling Alone page.

**Q: Will we be seated together?**
A: Children under 13 are seated next to an adult on the same booking free of charge.

**Q: Can I bring a stroller?**
A: Yes, you can use it up to the aircraft door and it is returned at the door on arrival.
