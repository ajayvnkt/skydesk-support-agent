# Choosing and Paying for Seats

## Seat types

- **Standard:** regular economy seat.
- **Preferred:** standard seat in the first rows of economy, closer to the exit.
- **Extra legroom:** at least 15 cm more legroom, including exit rows.

## Seat prices by fare class (per seat, per flight)

| Fare class | Standard | Preferred | Extra legroom |
|---|---|---|---|
| SkyLite | $12 (or free random seat at check-in) | $25 | $45 |
| SkyClassic | Free | $25 | $45 |
| SkyFlex | Free | Free | $45 |
| SkyBusiness | Business cabin seats, all free | n/a | n/a |

SkyMiles Silver members and above get free standard seats on every fare.

## How to choose a seat

1. **Manage booking > Seats**, or the seat step during check-in.
2. Tap a passenger, then tap an available seat. Prices show on the seat.
3. Tap **Confirm** and pay if required.

## Exit rows

Passengers in exit rows must be at least **15 years old**, able to help in an emergency, and not travelling with an infant or pet. Staff may move passengers who do not meet these conditions; the seat fee is then refunded.

## Families

Children under 13 are seated next to at least one accompanying adult on the same booking free of charge, even on SkyLite. If the app does not show adjacent seats, contact us before check-in.

## Seat refunds

Seat fees are refunded if SkyDesk moves you to a seat of a lower type, if the flight is cancelled, or if you cancel within the 24-hour risk-free window. They are not refunded if you choose not to travel.
