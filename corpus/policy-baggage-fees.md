# Baggage Fees

Prices are per bag, per passenger, per direction, in US dollars. "Online" means bought in the SkyDesk app or on skydesk.com up to 4 hours before departure. After that, airport prices apply.

## Extra checked bags (up to 23 kg each)

| Bag beyond your included allowance | Online | At the airport |
|---|---|---|
| 1st extra checked bag | $40 | $70 |
| 2nd extra checked bag | $60 | $90 |
| 3rd extra bag and beyond | $120 | $120 |

A passenger can travel with a maximum of 5 checked bags in total.

## Cabin bag for SkyLite

SkyLite does not include a cabin bag. You can add one cabin bag for **$35 online** or **$60 at the gate**.

## Overweight and oversize

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Overweight: 23.1 kg to 32 kg | $75 per bag |
| Oversize: 158 cm to 203 cm total dimensions | $100 per bag |
| Over 32 kg or over 203 cm | Not accepted as checked baggage. Ship as cargo. |

SkyBusiness bags up to 32 kg are included and are not charged the overweight fee. A bag that is both overweight and oversize pays both fees.

## Sports equipment

Golf bags and skis count as one regular checked bag if within 23 kg and 203 cm, and the oversize fee is waived for them. Bicycles are charged a flat $100 per direction.

## Refunds of bag fees

Bag fees are refunded only if the flight is cancelled by SkyDesk, or if you did not travel and cancelled before departure on a refundable fare. Fees are not refunded for unused bags if you flew.
