# Refunds and Cancellations Policy

This policy explains what a customer receives when they cancel a SkyDesk ticket themselves (a voluntary cancellation). When SkyDesk cancels or significantly changes a flight, the Delay and Cancellation Compensation policy and the Schedule Changes policy apply instead, and every fare class is entitled to a full refund of the unused portion.

All refunds, credits and fee waivers require the customer to be verified first. See the Customer Identity Verification Policy.

## The 24-hour exception

Any ticket, including SkyLite, can be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of booking if the conditions in the 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation policy are met. Everything below applies once that window has passed.

## What each fare class receives on voluntary cancellation

| Fare class | Cancel before departure | Form of refund |
|---|---|---|
| SkyLite | Non-refundable. No travel credit. Government taxes and airport fees are refunded on request. | Original form of payment (taxes only) |
| SkyClassic | Travel credit for the fare value minus a $100 cancellation fee | SkyDesk travel credit |
| SkyFlex | Full refund, no fee | Original form of payment |
| SkyBusiness | Full refund, no fee | Original form of payment |

Notes:

- SkyClassic cancellations never produce a cash refund, only travel credit. If the fare value is $100 or less, the credit is $0 and the customer should be told this before they confirm.
- SkyLite tax refunds must be requested within 12 months of the original travel date.
- Travel credits follow the rules in the Vouchers and Travel Credits policy, including their expiry.

## The refund cap

A refund can never exceed the total fare paid for that ticket, meaning the base fare plus taxes and carrier charges actually paid by the customer. This is a hard limit for every channel and every agent. It applies even when the customer was given a promotion, paid partly with miles, or is very unhappy.

- Ancillaries (seats, extra bags, pets, unaccompanied minor service) are refunded separately and only when SkyDesk did not provide the service.
- Compensation for delays is issued as travel credit or miles under its own policy. It is not a refund and is not added to the refund.
- Third-party travel insurance is never refunded by SkyDesk; the customer must contact the insurer.
- Where part of the fare was paid with a travel credit, that part is returned as a travel credit, not as cash.

If a customer asks for more than the fare paid (for example "refund me double for the trouble"), the agent explains the cap and, if the customer wants to pursue a complaint, offers the formal complaint route.

## No-shows

A ticket must be cancelled before the scheduled departure time. If the customer does not cancel and does not fly, the ticket becomes a no-show:

- SkyLite and SkyClassic: the ticket is forfeited. Taxes remain refundable on request.
- SkyFlex and SkyBusiness: the fare is converted to a travel credit minus a $100 no-show fee.

A no-show on one flight automatically cancels all later flights on the same booking. See the Missed Flights and No-Show policy.

## Processing times

| Original payment method | Refund time after approval |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | within 7 business days |
| PayPal or Apple Pay | within 7 business days |
| Bank transfer and all other methods | up to 20 business days |

Refunds always go back to the original form of payment. SkyDesk cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account, even if the original card has been cancelled; in that case the customer's bank forwards the funds.

## Partially used tickets

If the outbound flight has been flown and the customer cancels the return, the refund (or credit) is calculated on the unused portion only, using the same fare-class rules.

## Bookings made through travel agents

Tickets issued by a travel agency or online travel site must be refunded by that agency. SkyDesk agents can confirm the fare rules but cannot process the refund.

## Disputes in progress

If the customer has already opened a chargeback or card dispute for the booking, no refund or credit may be issued while the dispute is open. Hand the case to the Payments Integrity team as described in the Fraud and Chargebacks policy.
