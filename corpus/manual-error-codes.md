# App and Website Error Codes

When something goes wrong, the SkyDesk app and skydesk.com show an error code at the bottom of the message. Find the code below for the cause and the fix.

## SD-ERR-112: Payment declined

**What it means:** your card issuer declined the payment, or the 3-D Secure check (the confirmation in your banking app or by text) was not completed.

**How to fix it:**
1. Check the card number, expiry date, CVV and the billing ZIP/postal code. The billing address must match the one your bank holds.
2. Open your banking app and approve the payment if it asks you to. The 3-D Secure window times out after 5 minutes.
3. Make sure the card allows online and international transactions, and has enough available credit.
4. Try a different card, PayPal or Apple Pay.

Your fare and seats are held for **20 minutes** after the error, so you will not lose the price while you try again. After **3 declined attempts** in a row the card is blocked for SkyDesk payments for 24 hours to protect you from fraud; use a different payment method instead. SkyDesk agents cannot see why your bank declined the payment; only your bank can tell you.

## PNR-404: Booking not found

**What it means:** the booking reference and last name you entered do not match a booking.

**How to fix it:**
1. Check the 6-character booking reference on your confirmation email. SkyDesk references never contain the digits 0 or 1 or the letters O or I, so if you see a round character it is the letter Q or D, and a straight line is the letter L or J.
2. Enter the last name exactly as on the booking, without titles (Mr, Dr) and without spaces or hyphens for double-barrelled names (for example SMITHJONES).
3. If you booked through a travel agent or online travel site, wait up to **2 hours** after booking for the reservation to appear in SkyDesk systems.
4. If your ticket was issued by a partner airline (codeshare), use the partner's booking reference on the partner's website.

If none of these work, contact us with your e-ticket number (13 digits starting with 579).

## BAG-301: Allowance exceeded

**What it means:** the bag you are trying to add goes beyond what the system can sell online. This happens when:
- you try to add more than **5 checked bags** for one passenger (the maximum), or
- you enter a bag weight above **32 kg**, or
- a SkyLite passenger tries to add a second cabin bag (only one can be bought).

**How to fix it:** reduce the number of bags or split the weight across bags. Items over 32 kg cannot travel as baggage and must be shipped as cargo. For sports equipment or anything unusual, contact the contact center.

## CHK-210: Check-in not open yet

Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure. Try again when the countdown in the app reaches zero. If the error persists inside the 24-hour window, your booking may need to be checked in at the airport (for example unaccompanied minors or pets).

## SEAT-409: Seat no longer available

Someone else selected the seat while you were choosing. Refresh the seat map and choose another seat. You have not been charged.

## ACC-401: Session expired

You have been signed out after 30 minutes of inactivity. Sign in again; items in your basket are kept for 20 minutes.

## OTP-429: Too many code requests

You requested more than 5 one-time codes in 15 minutes. Wait **15 minutes** before requesting a new code. Check your spam folder and that the email or phone on your profile is correct.

## Still stuck?

Take a screenshot of the error, including the code and the time, and contact us through in-app chat.
