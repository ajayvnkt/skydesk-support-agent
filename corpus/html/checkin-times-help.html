Check-in & Boarding Times

Don’t miss your flight – please note these deadlines. They are measured from the scheduled departure time.

DomesticInternational
Online check-in opens24 hours24 hours
Online & kiosk check-in closes60 minutes90 minutes
Bag drop closes45 minutes60 minutes
Boarding gate closes15 minutes20 minutes

Where to check in

  • In the SkyDesk app (see the app guide “Checking in with the app”).
  • On skydesk.com.
  • At airport kiosks – you’ll need your booking reference or passport.
  • At the check-in desk, for unaccompanied minors, pets and special assistance.

Why can’t I check in online?

Online check-in isn’t available if your booking includes an unaccompanied minor, a pet, a stretcher, or if your travel documents need to be checked in person. Please go to the check-in desk.

Gates close strictly on time. Customers arriving after the gate has closed will not be boarded.
