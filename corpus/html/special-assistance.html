Special Assistance & Accessibility

We’re here to help every customer travel comfortably. All assistance services are free of charge.

Requesting assistance

Please tell us at least 48 hours before departure in Manage booking › Special assistance or through the contact center. We’ll still do our best for late requests.

Wheelchair assistance types

  • WCHR – you can walk short distances and climb stairs but need help over long distances.
  • WCHS – you can’t manage stairs but can walk to your seat.
  • WCHC – you are fully immobile and need help to your seat.

Mobility aids & medical devices

Wheelchairs, walkers, crutches and other mobility aids are carried free and do not count against your baggage allowance.

  • Battery-powered wheelchairs: tell us 48 hours before departure. Lithium-ion batteries must not exceed 300 Wh; you may carry one spare up to 300 Wh or two spares up to 160 Wh each in the cabin.
  • Portable oxygen concentrators (POC) must be FAA-approved and requested at least 72 hours before departure. Bring batteries for 150% of the flight time.
  • CPAP machines may be carried in addition to your cabin allowance.

Other services

  • Customers who are blind or have low vision, or are deaf or hard of hearing, can request a personal briefing from the crew.
  • A non-travelling companion can request an escort pass at the check-in desk to accompany you to the gate.
  • If you need an extra seat for medical reasons, contact us before booking.
