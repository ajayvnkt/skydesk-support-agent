Children Travelling Alone (Unaccompanied Minors)

Our Unaccompanied Minor (UM) service means a SkyDesk crew member looks after your child from check-in until they’re handed over at arrival.

Who needs the service?

Age on the day of travelTravelling alone
Under 5Not permitted – must travel with an adult aged 16+
5–11UM service mandatory
12–15UM service optional (on request)
16+Travels as an adult

Fee

The UM service costs $120 each way per booking, in addition to the fare. One fee covers up to 2 siblings travelling together on the same booking.

Restrictions

  • Nonstop flights only – no connections.
  • Not available on the last scheduled flight of the day to a destination, or on flights departing between 21:00 and 05:00.
  • UM bookings must be made through the contact center, not online.

On the day

  1. Arrive at the check-in desk at least 2 hours before departure with the completed UM-1 form.
  2. The adult dropping off must show photo ID and stay at the airport until the flight has taken off.
  3. The adult collecting the child must be the person named on the UM-1 form and show matching photo ID. We will not release the child to anyone else.

If the collecting adult is not there, the child stays in SkyDesk’s care and we contact the parent or guardian; any costs are charged to the booking.
