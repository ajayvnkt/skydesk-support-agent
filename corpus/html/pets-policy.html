Travelling with Pets

Small cats and dogs are welcome in the cabin on SkyDesk flights — here’s what you need to know.

Pets in the cabin

  • Only cats and dogs are accepted, and they must be at least 10 weeks old.
  • The pet and its carrier together must weigh no more than 8 kg.
  • Use a soft-sided, leak-proof carrier no larger than 45 × 35 × 20 cm. It must fit under the seat in front and stay closed for the whole flight.
  • One pet carrier per passenger, and a maximum of 4 pets in the cabin per flight, so book early.
  • The pet carrier replaces your cabin bag. You can still bring your personal item.

Fees & booking

ServiceFee
Pet in cabin$95 per pet, each way
Trained service dogFree

Pets must be added at least 48 hours before departure by calling the contact center; they can’t be added in the app. The pet fee is refunded only if SkyDesk cancels the flight.

Pets in the hold

SkyDesk does not carry pets in the hold or as checked baggage. For larger animals please use a specialist pet shipping company.

Service animals

Trained service dogs travel free in the cabin on all fares. Submit the U.S. DOT Service Animal Air Transportation Form at least 48 hours before departure through Manage booking › Special assistance. Emotional support animals are treated as pets and the pet rules & fee apply.

Tip: Snub-nosed breeds (e.g. pugs, bulldogs, Persian cats) are welcome in the cabin.
