Contact Us

Most questions can be answered right away by our help-center assistant in the app or at skydesk.com – it’s available 24/7 and can connect you to a person.

Ways to reach us

ChannelHoursBest for

Before you call

Have your 6-character booking reference and the last name on the booking ready. For any change, refund or credit we’ll need to verify you.

Hearing or speech impaired? Use the relay service of your choice to call our contact center.

Social media

We can answer general questions on social media but we never handle bookings or ask for payment details there.
