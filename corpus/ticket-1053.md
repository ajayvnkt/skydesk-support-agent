# Ticket #1053: can i bring my cat

**Channel:** in-app chat | **Fare:** SkyClassic

**Customer message:**
> hey can i bring my cat on the plane w me? shes about 5kg with her carier. flight is in 3 days, i dont see option in the app

**Agent resolution:**
Confirmed cats are accepted in the cabin when pet and carrier together are no more than 8 kg, so 5 kg is fine, and the carrier must be soft-sided and no larger than 45 x 35 x 20 cm. Explained pets cannot be added in the app and must be added by calling the contact center at least 48 hours before departure; the fee is $95 each way, and the carrier takes the place of her cabin bag. Customer called the contact center the same day.

**Resolution code:** RES-INFO
