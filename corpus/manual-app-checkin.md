# Checking In with the SkyDesk App

Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure. The exact closing times for domestic and international flights are on the Check-in & Boarding Times page.

## Step by step

1. Open the app and tap **Trips**. Your upcoming trip appears automatically if you are signed in to the SkyMiles account used to book. Otherwise tap **Add a trip** and enter the booking reference and last name.
2. Tap **Check in**. Select the passengers you want to check in. You can check in everyone on the booking at once.
3. **International flights:** scan the photo page of each passport with the camera, or type the details. Check the expiry date carefully.
4. Answer the dangerous goods question (lithium batteries, flammables).
5. Review or choose seats. SkyLite passengers who have not paid for a seat are assigned one automatically at this step.
6. Add bags if you need to (see Adding bags in the app).
7. Tap **Get boarding pass**. Your boarding pass appears in the app and can be added to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

## If you have checked bags

After checking in online, drop your bags at the **Bag drop** counter at the airport before the bag-drop deadline. Bag tags are printed at the counter or at self-service kiosks.

## Common problems

- **The Check in button is greyed out:** check-in has not opened yet, or your booking must be checked in at the desk (pets, unaccompanied minors, special documents). See error CHK-210.
- **"Documents must be verified at the airport":** your passport needs a manual check. Your seat is held; go to the check-in desk.
- **Group of more than 9 passengers:** check in in two batches.

## Cancelling check-in

If you have checked in but cannot travel, tap **Trips > your trip > Undo check-in** before departure. Otherwise the flight counts as flown or as a no-show.
