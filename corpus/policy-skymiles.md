# SkyMiles Loyalty Programme

SkyMiles is free to join in the app or on skydesk.com. Add your SkyMiles number to a booking at any time up to check-in to earn miles.

## Earning miles

Miles are earned per US dollar of base fare plus carrier charges (not government taxes):

| Fare class | Miles per $1 |
|---|---|
| SkyLite | 2 |
| SkyClassic | 5 |
| SkyFlex | 7 |
| SkyBusiness | 10 |

Miles appear in your account within 72 hours after the flight. Missing miles can be claimed up to 6 months after the flight with your booking reference.

## Status tiers

Status is based on miles earned in a calendar year:

| Tier | Miles needed per year | Key benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 25,000 | Priority check-in, free standard seats on all fares |
| Gold | 50,000 | Silver benefits, plus one extra checked bag of up to 23 kg on every flight, and priority boarding |
| Platinum | 100,000 | Gold benefits, plus lounge access and free same-day changes on any fare except SkyLite |

The Gold extra bag is added on top of the fare's included allowance, for the member only, not for companions.

## Using miles

Miles can pay for flights (from 7,500 miles one way), seats and bags. A booking paid fully with miles follows SkyClassic change and refund rules, and refunded miles return to the account.

## Expiry

Miles expire after **24 months without any account activity**. Earning or redeeming any miles resets the 24 months. Status members' miles do not expire while their status is active.

## Account security

SkyMiles accounts are protected by a one-time code at login on new devices. If you see redemptions you did not make, contact us straight away (see Fraud, Unauthorized Bookings and Chargebacks).
