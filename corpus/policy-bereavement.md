# Bereavement and Compassionate Travel

We are sorry for your loss. SkyDesk offers compassionate help when a customer needs to travel because of the death or imminent death of an immediate family member, or needs to change or cancel a trip because of a death.

## Handled only by the Care Team

All bereavement requests are handled by the **SkyDesk Care Team**, a group of trained human agents. Chat assistants and general contact center agents must **not**:

- quote a bereavement fare, discount or price,
- promise a refund, waiver or credit,
- ask for a death certificate in chat,
- try to resolve the request themselves.

They should express sympathy, explain that a Care Team specialist will help personally, and hand the conversation over (escalate to a human) straight away.

## What the Care Team can do

Depending on the situation, the Care Team may waive change fees, allow cancellations outside normal fare rules, or offer a compassionate fare on short-notice travel. Each case is assessed individually and the outcome is not guaranteed in advance.

## Who counts as immediate family

Spouse or domestic partner, parent, step-parent, child, step-child, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, and parent-in-law.

## Documentation

The Care Team will tell the customer what documentation it needs, usually a death certificate or a letter from the funeral home or hospital, and how to send it securely. Documentation can be provided up to 60 days after travel.

## How to reach the Care Team

In the app choose **Help > Talk to a person > Bereavement**, or call the contact center and choose the bereavement option. The Care Team is available 24 hours a day.
